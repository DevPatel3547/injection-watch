InjectionWatch options

Local scanning

Every page you open is scanned on your device for hidden text aimed at AI agents. The badge shows the number of findings. Nothing about your browsing leaves the browser because of scanning.

Reporting (off by default)

When this is on, each finding in the popup gets a Report button. Nothing is sent until you click it and confirm a dialog showing the exact data. A report contains only: the page URL without query string or fragment, a SHA-256 hash of the hidden text, its category and technique, and the extension version. Never page text, excerpts, cookies, or identifiers. Reports from incognito windows are refused. Reports are leads: nothing is published until the project's crawler independently re-observes the payload on the public page.

Saving an endpoint asks Chrome for permission to contact that one origin, and nothing else.
